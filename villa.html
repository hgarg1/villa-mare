The villa

Architecture

Quiet materials, open walls

Travertine, teak and linen, chosen to soften in the salt air rather than fight it. Every principal room opens fully to the terrace so the breeze does the work of air-conditioning for most of the year.

Single-storey living with a sheltered upper sun deck. The beach is twelve steps from the pool.
